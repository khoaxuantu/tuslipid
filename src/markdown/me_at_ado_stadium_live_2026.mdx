Hmm, where should I start? 🤔

On a random day in November 2025, as usual, I was devastated scrolling down my YouTube homepage to 
seek a song that could chill out my stress. To describe how hard it was, I would say it was an
infinite scroll. There was a moment when the CPop was not enough for me; the US country-pop songs 
were not enough as well; and the VPop was flooded with autotuned rap and drunk-indie vocals 
arghhhhhhhhhhhhhh; I hate it.

Suddenly, I found out about [Ado's live version of the *唱 (Show)*](https://youtu.be/Igr6jQJEoNs).
I was... literally blown away by her vocals; all of my stresses were literally released. 😭 That's 
how I became her fan. On that day, I swear to myself that I have to go to her live concert at least 
one time in my life, lol.

Then 2026 came with me, with full of surprises. I saw a lottery for Japan-overseas guests included
a ticket for her concert at Nissan Stadium in July. I applied it, and I won it, lol.

Immediately after knowing that I had won the lottery, I started to plan for my trip to Japan. It 
was a 5 days 4 nights trip, soon it became my most satisfying trip so far. 

At that time, I did not recognize how lucky I was...

## About the concert

> Ado STADIUM LIVE 2026 "Ao"

That's the title of the concert that I attended. It was held at Nissan Stadium, Yokohama, on 2 days,
the 4th and 5th July. Luckily enough, I was able to visit both days.

For the tickets, there are a total of 7 tiers, including VIP, SS, S, A, B, Family, and Wheelchair.
The VIP and SS seats took place on the stadium's field, right before the stage. The other tickets 
were located in the stadium's stands.

An inviolable rule at every her concert is that photos, videos, recordings, and binoculars are 
strictly prohibited inside the venue. It is for protecting Ado's privacy. On the stage, she always
performs as a silhouette and never reveals her face.

Therefore, all the pictures that I attach in this post were only taken when I was outside the venue
or borrowed from the gallery of the staff.

## Me on day one

The first day of the concert, or, I would say, the first time I attended an Ado concert.

Honestly, I have no experience visiting a concert before. I entered the stadium and wondered what I
should do next. LMAO.

You know what? I entered the venue directly without checking the merchandise booths and the gacha
booths. It turns out that it was a big mistake... I listened to her on the first day without waving 
any light stick. 😭

Following up on my winning prize, I got the S ticket for the first day only. I was placed in a 
position that faced the main stage directly, providing one of the best views from the stands.

## Me on day two

The next day, I had to purchase the on-site ticket, which only allowed me to select the B ticket 
type. I ended up on the top corner of the stands.

But surprisingly, I discovered a beautiful hidden gem while sitting on this seat: the whole stadium 
with the lights. It was overwhelming, I swear.

Learning from yesterday's experience, I headed to the merchandise booth right after getting my
ticket and finally got myself a lightstick.

For those who may not know, Ado produced an idol group in 2024, called Phantom Siita.

### The orchestra symphony

The moment the violin sound began, I was almost shouting over the moon.

I love listening to orchestra symphonies. Since the first time I heard Jacob Collier's audience choir 
and orchestra improvised videos, I have learnt how much strong emotion the live instruments can 
release.

One day, I stopped by Wagakki Band's Symphonic Night on YouTube, e.g., 
[*“Bring me to life” ft. Amy Lee*](https://youtu.be/hk1GtYK7BJM) or 
[*千本桜 (Senbonzakura)*](https://youtu.be/FFK4AK8pHj8), which was backed by an orchestra. The 
combination pf the symphony and the music band soon became one of my favourites. I swear to you that
I did ask whether I had a chance to listen to Ado sing with an orchestra afterwards.

She did fulfil my wish at "Ao"!!!

Yeah, I'm not delusional; she did. She brought an orchestra to us; they did play. That was a real

### The encore

This was the first time ever I experienced an encore chant. As my friend's explanation, a concert 
*encore* is an extra performance at the end of the concert, right after the main setlist, when the
artist leaves the stage and then returns to play more songs in response to the crowd's cheers and 
claps.

At "Ao", after Ado's first MC, she left the stage, and the light turned off. The stadium started to
chant, "Encore... encore... encore...".

The encore on day 2 was even more special to me. The people around my stand initially were not so
enthusiastic in chanting. There were only a few dudes who did try to raise the beat. Noticing the chant 
was weakening gradually, I boldly joined in, and it went far more than I expected. As the 
design of the stadium stands, my voice can carry very far. Gradually, on both my left and right,
more and more people started to chant. 

I screamed "Encore", they screamed "Ankōru"... Then the light blew up.

Ado appeared on a trolley, drove an U-shaped path around the stadium, and got close to the audience.
Following up, she sang 
[*おどるポンポコリン (Odoru Poponkorin)*](https://youtu.be/TZYqDl19qLQ),
[*Love me forever!*](https://youtu.be/WSuAVkaSC38),
[*アタシは問題作 (I'm controversy)*](https://youtu.be/lZGo8jcEW2M), and
[*FREEDOM*](https://youtu.be/hgyGhu49sGc).

### The *Vivarium* and fireworks

Ado marked the last song by her featured track
[*ビバリウム (Vivarium)*](https://youtu.be/YvYii6dluM8). It is one of two songs so far that she 
has composed by herself. The song was released in February 2026, along with her autobiographical
novel *Vivarium: Ado and Me*.

For Ado and the Adomins, I believe the whole concert was designed to end with this song. As 
the concert title - "Ao" - is also the name of the main character in her novel, which adds 
significance to the performance.

The song ended up with the silver confetti launched towards the area and numerous colorful
fireworks lit up the night sky.

I can share a post from X recorded outside the stadium during the fireworks launched:
[x.com](https://x.com/uITkxzrF1zV8YL/status/2073383462875349395).

## Next?

"Ao" was the first concert that I planned and traveled to by myself, the first time I went to Japan, 
and the first time I saw my favorite artist performing. More than 20000 steps per day in Yokohama 
and Tokyo. 3700 kilometers of traveling distance, from Hanoi to Tokyo.

It has opened up many concert experiences for me in the future.

In the live's MC, Ado told us that she was planning a new world tour as well. When that tour
launches, I will definitely go see her again. 

Taiwan has to be great; I have been so familiar with Taipei after 7 years living there. A trip to
reminisce about my college days plus a concert is decent. 

Additionally, there are also more cities that I can go to, such as Bangkok, Manila, Singapore, Hong 
Kong, or Seoul. Man, I just love traveling to new places...

Currently, I will be back to my normal life, keep following Ado remotely, and look forward
to new journeys in the future.
